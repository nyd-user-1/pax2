The scoring framework: how engagement and persuasion are quantified, weighted, and connected to real outcomes

EngagementOutlier Score: a short's views over its own channel's median views. Outperformed 3x: an Outlier Score of 3 or more once settled. Channel size leaves the comparison.
Resonance, Gen 1A pre-publication probability that a video outperforms 3x, stacked from three instruments: the outlier classifier's calibrated probability (qc), the craft rank within the channel (qx), the headline percentile (qh).
Weightsb0 0.193 · bc 0.157 · bx 0.105 · bh 0.155, fit on 1,240 held-out-channel baseline rows
PersuasionPer-trait effects from 146 randomized ad studies (433 favorability and 575 vote-choice estimates): plain-folks appeal +0.79 points of vote choice (0.32 to 1.25); an explicit ask to vote +0.59 (0.19 to 0.99).
Outcome linkEarly-hit predictor, views in the first 6 hours to outperformed 3x at 72: AUC 0.729 pooled, 0.862 within channel. Live.

Prove that it works, explain its limits

Back-test against historical contentEvery gate scores held-out data. Resonance is validated on 10 held-out channels that the classifier and the pair picker never trained on: AUC 0.598 against chance at 0.5.
Compare predictions with actual resultsEarly-hit predictor: 6-hour AUC 0.729 pooled, 0.862 within channel. Paid-flight loop: the pick outperformed 3x in 7 of 8 daily slates, against a 0.207 hit rate for a uniform pick; picking the highest 6-hour views over median scores the same.
Check against experimental (RCT) evidence146 randomized ad studies. Single traits carry measured differences; an ad's full trait set does not predict its effect in a study it was not fit on (leave-one-study-out R² −0.001 on vote choice).
Registered before fitEach learning loop's evaluation and pass rule is committed, timestamped, before its fit, and run without change. Of the eight loops evaluated, two did not pass; four cold-start loops wait for logged decisions.
Failures keptTranscript words did not predict an outlier (0.597 against 0.624); the video embedding added nothing over text (0.636 against 0.648); posting cadence carried no signal beyond channel context.
ObservationalLogged publish choices were never randomized; the learning loops estimate which choices co-occur with outperforming, not what a channel gains by switching. Unlock: randomized arms on a partner's own publishes, logged with their propensities.
TransferThe persuasion effects come from 2018 and 2020 ads, mostly presidential; the transfer to 2026 shorts is untested. Unlock: a partner experiment with a control group.
