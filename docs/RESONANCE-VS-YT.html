The Resonance formula

Engage = 3-second plays ÷ impressions

Interest = average view duration ÷ video length

Act = (shares + added to playlists + comments) ÷ 3-second plays

Resonance = 0.25 × Engage score + 0.40 × Interest score + 0.35 × Act score

Each rate is evidence-weighted before scoring — (count + k × reference average) ÷ (base + k), with k = 2,000 impressions for Engage, 300 viewers for Interest, 800 plays for Act — then measured as its distance from the reference average on the log-odds scale and mapped to 0–100, where 50 is the reference average of the first round of paid results. On YouTube, 3-second plays = video starts × retention at 0:03.
